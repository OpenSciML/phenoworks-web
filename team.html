The people behind PhenoWorks

Building better tools for research, together.

PhenoWorks brings together researchers across disciplines to build reusable tools for crop phenotyping and data analysis.

Scientific direction and project coordination.

Project Lead / Principal Investigator

Muthu Bagavathiannan

Billie Turner Professor of Agronomy

Texas A&M University

Research focused on precision agronomy, plant phenotyping, and novel and precision weed management technologies

Development Lead

Software architecture and platform development.

Lead Developer

Henry Ruiz

Research Scientist

Texas A&M AgriLife Research

Creator and lead developer of PhenoWorks and LgoPy, with a research focus on leveraging the emerging capabilities of Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs), including reasoning, planning, and multi-agent collaboration, to streamline analytical workflows for multimodal remote sensing data. His work integrates ground-based field observations, UAV imagery, satellite data, LiDAR, and Ground Penetrating Radar (GPR) to advance scientific research automation, agricultural digital phenotyping, precision agriculture, and AI-driven data analysis.
